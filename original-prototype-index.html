THE METHOD

How the demo strategy works

01

Generate sample candles

The prototype creates an illustrative price series for each pair. It is not connected to a broker or market feed.

02

Compare moving averages

A fast EMA and slow EMA are compared. A recent crossover creates a BUY or SELL direction; otherwise the signal is WAIT.

03

Calculate risk levels

Demo entry, stop-loss, and take-profit levels are calculated using a fixed illustrative distance. Test thoroughly before real use.
